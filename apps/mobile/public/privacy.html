PMAN — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

PMAN (“the app”) is a property-management app for landlords and property managers, with a portal for their tenants. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what control you have over it.

The short version. PMAN collects only the information needed to manage a tenancy: your account details, the rental records your property manager keeps, and any files you choose to upload. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, and your data is never sold or shared for advertising. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

Who is responsible for your data

The app is operated by the individual or business that runs this installation of PMAN (“we”, “us”). Contact: rronyy001@gmail.com.

If you are a tenant, your property manager decides what tenancy records are kept about you and is the controller of that information; we process it on their behalf. Questions about your tenancy records are best directed to your property manager first.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account details — name, email address, phone number (optional), and a password To create your account, sign you in, and secure it. Passwords are stored only as a salted bcrypt hash; we never store or see your actual password.
Tenancy records — property and unit details, lease dates and terms, rent amounts, payments, balances, late fees, and an optional emergency contact These are the core function of the app: tracking who rents what and who has paid. Entered by the property manager.
Files you upload — payment screenshots or receipts, maintenance photos, and lease or tenancy documents So a payment can be verified and approved, a repair can be understood, and lease paperwork is available to both sides.
Messages — messages between a tenant and their property manager, and property announcements To deliver in-app communication about the tenancy.
Notification token — a push token identifying your device install To send you alerts (for example, that a payment was submitted, approved, or declined). Only stored if you allow notifications.

What we do not collect

  • No payment card, bank account, or financial credentials. PMAN does not process card payments. Tenants pay by whatever method they already use (bank transfer, Zelle, cash, cheque) outside the app and simply record it here for approval.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, calendar, microphone, or background camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers, ad networks, or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Photo and file access

The app asks for permission to access your photos or files only at the moment you tap to attach one. It reads only the specific file you pick. It does not browse, scan, or upload your photo library.

How your data is used and shared

Your information is used solely to operate the app. Specifically:

  • A property manager can see the records and uploads relating to their own properties, tenants and leases.
  • A tenant can see only their own lease, balance, payments, documents and messages.
  • We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it for advertising or marketing.

We share data with third parties only where strictly necessary to run the service:

  • Hosting provider — stores the database and uploaded files so the app can run.
  • Expo push notification service — delivers push notifications to your device, if you enable them.
  • Email delivery provider — sends transactional emails such as a payment approval notice, where email is configured.
  • Legal — if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost 12) and are never stored in readable form.
  • Sessions expire, and changing your password or signing out everywhere immediately invalidates every existing session on all devices.
  • Uploaded files are not publicly accessible: they are served only through short-lived, signed links to users authorised to see them.
  • Sign-in and account endpoints are rate-limited to resist brute-force attempts.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of tenancy and payment records.

How long we keep it

Tenancy records are kept while the account is active, because rent ledgers and lease history need to remain auditable for both parties. When you delete your account, your personal account data is removed as described below. Property managers may retain lease and payment history where they are required to for tax, accounting, or legal reasons.

Deleting your account and data

In the app:

  • Property managers: open More → Delete account.
  • Tenants: open Account → Delete account.

You will be asked to re-enter your password to confirm. This permanently deletes your login, your profile details, your push notification tokens, and your in-app notifications, and immediately signs you out of every device. It cannot be undone.

By email: if you cannot access the app, email rronyy001@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will action it within 30 days.

Note for tenants: deleting your portal login removes your access and personal login data. Your property manager may still keep the underlying tenancy record (lease dates, rent charged and paid) as the landlord’s own business record, as they are generally entitled and sometimes legally required to do.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these, email rronyy001@gmail.com. Tenants should contact their property manager first, as they control the tenancy record.

Children

PMAN is a business tool intended for adults entering rental agreements. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that threshold).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app.
